## What it does

The plugin helps you write search strings for literature databases. It does not run searches, access your library's subscriptions, or guarantee any particular results.

## Check your results

Search strings are generated by AI and may contain errors. Database syntax and subject lists also change over time. Test every string in the database before relying on it, especially for systematic or scoping reviews. For important searches, have the strategy reviewed by a librarian.
